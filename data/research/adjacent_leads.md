Research-institute scientist, outside the primary US-university-faculty roster and counts. Verified 2026-10-01T00:58:56Z; first discovered 2026-10-01T00:55:42Z.

**Role:** Associate Scientist, Applied Ocean Physics and Engineering; WARPLab PI. Public professional email: yogi@whoi.edu.

**Fit:** Learned depth, DINOv2 features, multimodal SLAM, 3D Gaussian reconstruction and probabilistic active exploration on underwater robots. This is an adjacent marine embodied-perception direction, not primarily social HRI.

**Physical evidence:** April 2026 ReefMapGS uses CUREE reef surveys covering 347.3 m and 695.5 m. The 2025 agreement accommodates no compensation from WHOI or home sources, but that does not establish wholly personal immigration funding.
