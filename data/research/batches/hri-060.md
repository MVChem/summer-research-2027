## Jose Luis Contreras-Vidal · University of Houston

- 方向：Deep-learning neural decoding；Brain-machine interfaces；Powered exoskeletons；Real-time artifact suppression；Human-robot interaction
- 匹配理由：Substantive AI methods include trainable neural denoising, spatial/time-frequency feature extraction and recurrent gait-state decoding, tied to physical exoskeleton control. The work is specialized biomedical robotics; simulation, signal processing or existing-data evaluation may be more realistic to scope than participant-facing work.
- 真机证据（public-hardware-evidence）：The full August 2026 pilot deploys a neural feature-extraction block plus PolyTVL/LSTM gait decoder on an actual 10-actuator Rex lower-limb exoskeleton. A Python control system triggers steps from EEG predictions; one healthy participant completed five open-loop and five mixed open/closed-loop sessions. Rex-assisted initiation succeeded in 55.3% of trials, with 18.2% false positives. Mean inference including preprocessing was 70.5 ms, while valid end-to-end initiation latency was about 1.04 seconds. This is physical learned control, but remains a single-subject feasibility study, not demonstrated clinical effectiveness or broadly reliable autonomous walking.
