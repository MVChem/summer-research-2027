DBO performs better in later validation stages, but both methods have residual error and no significant difference in secondary propulsion outcomes. This is single-parameter laboratory training with healthy adults, not demonstrated clinical efficacy after stroke, generalized whole-body control, or an autonomous outdoor wearable policy. First author GilHwan Kim is independently verified as Sergi’s lab doctoral graduate in April 2026.
